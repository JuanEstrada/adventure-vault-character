# Dwarf

## Creature Type: Humanoid

## Size: Medium (about 4–5 feet tall)

## Speed: 30 feet

As a Dwarf, you have these special traits. Darkvision. You have Darkvision with a range of 120 feet. Dwarven Resilience. You have Resistance to Poison damage. You also have Advantage on sav - ing throws you make to avoid or end the Poisoned condition. Dwarven Toughness. Your Hit Point maximum increases by 1, and it increases by 1 again whenever you gain a level. Stonecunning.  As a Bonus Action, you gain Trem - orsense with a range of 60 feet for 10 minutes. You must be on a stone surface or touching a stone sur - face to use this Tremorsense. The stone can be natu - ral or worked. You can use this Bonus Action a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
