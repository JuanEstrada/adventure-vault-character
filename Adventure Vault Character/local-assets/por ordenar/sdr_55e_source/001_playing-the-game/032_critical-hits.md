# Critical Hits

When you score a Critical Hit, you deal extra dam - age. Roll the attack’s damage dice twice, add them together, and add any relevant modifiers as normal. For example, if you score a Critical Hit with a Dag - ger, roll 2d4 for the damage rather than 1d4, and add your relevant ability modifier. If the attack in - volves other damage dice, such as from the Rogue’s Sneak Attack feature, you also roll those dice twice.
